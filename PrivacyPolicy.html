🔍 Privacy overview

OmniUnit: Ultimate Converter is a powerful, lightning-fast unit converter for everyday use — from length and weight to currency, temperature, data storage, and speed. We value your privacy and believe in complete transparency. This policy explains what data we collect, why we need it, and how you stay in full control.

Our commitment: No selling of personal data, minimal data collection, full compliance with global privacy laws (GDPR/CCPA). Your trust is the core of our unit conversion engine.

📊 1. What data we collect

OmniUnit is designed to be lightweight and privacy-first. We collect only anonymous, non‑personal information to improve app performance and stability:

  • 📱 Device & technical info — model, OS version, language, country, advertising identifier (AAID / IDFA), IP address (anonymized). Used for analytics & ads.
  • ⚙️ App usage & preferences — last used categories, favorite conversions, theme preference (dark/light). Stored locally on your device.
  • 📢 Ad interaction data — via Google AdMob. AdMob may use identifiers to serve contextual or non-personalized ads. No personal data is shared.
  • 💎 No account or personal info required — you can use all conversion features without creating an account or providing any personal details.

We never access your contacts, media, files, or precise location.

⚙️ 2. How we use your data

Your information helps us deliver the smoothest conversion experience:

  • Improve app performance & stability — using Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics to identify bugs and optimize speed.
  • Display relevant ads (optional interstitials and banners) via Google AdMob to keep the app free.
  • Save user preferences — like dark mode or favorite conversion units.
  • Aggregate usage statistics to understand which converters are most popular.
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

🔌 3. Third‑party services (SDKs)

To provide core functionalities, we integrate with trusted partners:

  • Google AdMob – advertising. May collect device data and IP for ad relevance. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics – anonymized crash reports and usage metrics. Helps us keep OmniUnit ultra‑stable.
  • Google Play Billing – if you choose to remove ads via in‑app purchase, Google Play processes the transaction. We never see payment details.

All third parties comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy frameworks.

🗑️ 4. Data retention & deletion

Your conversion history and settings remain on your device only. Uninstalling OmniUnit removes all local data. No server-side account exists. To request deletion of any analytics data linked to your advertising ID, email us at nadeemgamer180@gmail.com. We’ll respond within 30 days.

You can reset your advertising ID anytime: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.

🎯 5. Your ad choices

We respect your advertising preferences:

  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • You may also purchase the “Remove Ads” feature (one‑time IAP) to enjoy an ad‑free experience.
  • For users under the age of consent, only non‑personalized contextual ads are shown.
Child‑safe compliance (COPPA / GDPR‑K) enabled

🧸 6. Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR‑K)

OmniUnit: Ultimate Converter is intended for general audiences, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

  • If the Play Store identifies a child account, personalized ads are automatically disabled.
  • Parents: if you believe your child has provided any personal information (e.g., via customer support), contact us immediately and we will delete it.

🔒 7. Security measures

We implement industry‑standard security: encrypted network connections (TLS), minimal data retention, and restricted internal access. While no system is 100% invulnerable, we continuously update our practices to protect your information.

  • Access any personal data we might hold (linked to advertising ID).
  • Request correction or deletion of such data.
  • Opt out of “sale” of personal data (we never sell).
  • Withdraw consent for analytics by resetting your advertising ID.

To exercise any right, email nadeemgamer180@gmail.com with subject “Privacy Request – OmniUnit Converter”. We will respond promptly.
